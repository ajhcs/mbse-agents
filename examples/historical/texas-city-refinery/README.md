# Texas City refinery: measure the hazard you intend to control

**HG-13 · 2005 · industrial · failure**

Steady production and favorable injury statistics do not establish readiness for a hazardous startup.

All `HG-` identifiers are new teaching identifiers. Historical facts below
are attributed to sources; the boundary, decisions, artifact, questions, and
transfer exercise are newly authored. No local historical simulation was performed.

## Source-grounded facts

- **HG-13-F1:** The accident occurred during startup of the isomerization unit; a flooded distillation tower and atmospheric release exposed people near occupied trailers. [HG-13-S1](https://www.csb.gov/file.aspx?DocumentId=5596); Executive summary.
- **HG-13-F2:** The CSB found that reliance on low personal-injury rates gave a misleading picture of process-safety performance. [HG-13-S1](https://www.csb.gov/file.aspx?DocumentId=5596); Executive summary; section 1.6; section 9.3.

## Retrospective systems analysis

**Boundary.** Process equipment, startup state, instrumentation, staffing, temporary occupied buildings, maintenance, and management measures belong to the hazard boundary.

**Interfaces to examine**

- Operating mode → protection: startup changes inventories and actions relative to normal production.
- Management metric → resource decision: a convenient metric may omit the accident mechanism of concern.

**Decision.** Which evidence should support a startup readiness claim?

- Use recent production availability and personal-injury performance.
- Check mode-specific protection, instrumentation, procedures, staffing, and occupied-area exposure.

**Teaching recommendation.** Build measures around barriers to the major hazard and their observed condition.

**Uncertainty.** A barrier checklist still needs demonstrated functionality and authority to act on deficiencies.

## HG-13-A1: Startup readiness and indicator slice

| People in exposure area | Review temporary occupancy against credible releases | Siting and evacuation basis |
| Management measure | Track unresolved barrier impairments with consequence | Age, ownership, and verified closure of impairments |

## Practice engineering judgment

1. Why can a declining injury rate coexist with increasing major-accident risk?
2. What changes when a plant enters a transient operating mode?
3. Write a management brief that distinguishes output performance from protection health.

**Reasoning signals for a formative review**

- Links each measure to a mechanism and a decision.
- Includes temporary facilities and contractors in the system boundary.
- Avoids substituting completed paperwork for demonstrated protection.

**Transfer exercise.** Synthetic: a plant dashboard is green while three protective functions are bypassed. Redesign the decision-relevant portion of the dashboard.

**Limits.** The artifact is not a refinery operating procedure and does not establish safe staffing, siting distances, or protection settings.
