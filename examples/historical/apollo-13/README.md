# Apollo 13: reframe the mission after a loss

**HG-01 · 1970 · space · recovery**

Separate the original mission objective, the survival objective, and the resources that make a new operating concept feasible.

All `HG-` identifiers are new teaching identifiers. Historical facts below
are attributed to sources; the boundary, decisions, artifact, questions, and
transfer exercise are newly authored. No local historical simulation was performed.

## Source-grounded facts

- **HG-01-F1:** The planned lunar landing was abandoned following loss of service-module cryogenic oxygen; the lunar module supplied life support and power during the return. [HG-01-S1](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19710003598/downloads/19710003598.pdf); Summary.
- **HG-01-F2:** The command module was retained for Earth entry while the lunar module supported the crew before separation. [HG-01-S1](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19710003598/downloads/19710003598.pdf); Summary; command-module and lunar-module performance.

## Retrospective systems analysis

**Boundary.** Crew, both spacecraft modules, consumables, ground teams, communications, trajectory, and recovery forces form the return system.

**Interfaces to examine**

- Lunar module → crew: consumable capacity depends on duration and demand, not merely cabin occupancy.
- Ground team → crew: revised procedures compete for crew attention and need configuration control.

**Decision.** How should a team choose a new objective when the nominal mission is no longer feasible?

- Continue optimizing the original science plan.
- Establish a return objective, protected resources, and a controlled sequence of mode changes.

**Teaching recommendation.** Use a jointly maintained resource and mode ledger before optimizing individual subsystems.

**Uncertainty.** A plausible return sequence still needs time-dependent margins, operational rehearsal, and authoritative flight decisions.

## HG-01-A1: Contingency resource and mode ledger

This proposed artifact is a compact starting point for review. Its evidence

## Practice engineering judgment

1. Which original requirements remain binding after the objective changes?
2. How could saving power locally increase risk during entry preparation?
3. Give a 60-second status message distinguishing observed resources from predicted margin.

**Reasoning signals for a formative review**

- Names survival constraints and explicitly retires obsolete mission goals.
- Connects subsystem conservation to later modes and interfaces.
- Reports uncertainty and the next discriminating observation.

**Transfer exercise.** Synthetic: a remote habitat loses its primary power source. Draft a 12-hour resource ledger and identify the assumption that most threatens return to normal operation.

**Limits.** The ledger is newly authored. No Apollo trajectory, consumable, or crew-procedure simulation was performed.
