## Observed agent performance

[Engineering task review](../evals/evidence/performance-20261113/README.md)
found 23 of 24 outputs met every case criterion. One skill-assisted change-review
plan gave conflicting failure/inconclusive rules when uncertainty crossed a timing
limit. Its original output and partial rating remain preserved. The corresponding
baseline response avoided that ambiguity; the other four matched tasks tied at
the rubric ceiling.

[Historical project review](../evals/evidence/historical-performance-20261113/README.md)
found all three outputs met five assessed dimensions. The reviewer disclosed its
numeric anchors because the protocol named dimensions and critical errors but
had not predeclared a numeric scale.

[Targeted tuning](../evals/evidence/verification-tuning-20261113/README.md)
added consistent numerical decision rules and explicit uncertainty meanings to
the verification skill. Both prior and revised guidance succeeded on a new transfer
case. That supports the bounded repair's consistency but shows no incremental gain.

[Revised quiz runs](../evals/evidence/quiz-0.2.0-20261114/README.md)
scored 24/24 selections and 94/96 independently reviewed reasoning points in each
arm: no packages, task skills, and task-plus-domain skills. Every arm had the same
two partial reasoning criteria. The initial cueable bank and its misleadingly high
scores are [preserved as a diagnostic failure](../evals/evidence/quiz-diagnostic-20261113/README.md).
The revised selection score also reaches a ceiling; it is not a discriminating
measure of expert-level systems-engineering performance on this workload.

These are instruction-restricted exercises on a shared host. Exact served model,
settings and costs were unavailable; several cases share one producer context.
Skill reads and source retrievals are participant-reported and checked against
artifacts where possible. Separate agents reviewed the work; no human expert
acceptance or causal skill-effect estimate is claimed. Metadata amendments and
context limitations remain in the original records and assessments.

## Host and engineering limits

[Native Codex source discovery](evidence/native-codex-discovery.json) observed all 13
canonical namespaced entrypoints with codex-cli 0.169.0 and no model call. It does
not establish automatic behavioral activation or installation of the built plugin.
Claude Code and Databricks native workspace activation were not exercised because
those hosts were unavailable. [Host support](../platform/support.md) distinguishes
file-layout/export checks from native behavior.

Synthetic probabilities are conditional examples, not measured reliability.
The finite alarm and pump models do not establish arbitrary-time or hardware
behavior, physical performance, certification or risk acceptance. Fifteen other
domain baselines have corrected source/identity/evidence inventories but no new
executed system simulation. The gallery retains their substantive proposals and
specific evidence work; it does not relabel them as proven designs.
