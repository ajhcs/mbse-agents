## Located finding

**RR-01: Make failure and inconclusive timing outcomes mutually exclusive** — importance: medium (P2); repair effort: small: one acceptance paragraph and boundary examples; noncritical.

Location: [task-domain HO-13 verification.md, line 32](/workspace/worktrees/builderbob/mbse-performance-20261113-v2/task-domain/HO-13/outputs/verification.md:32). Line 32 calls a result above 200 ms a failure, then calls a result whose uncertainty straddles the limit inconclusive. For a measured latency of 201 ms with a 5 ms uncertainty bound, both sentences apply. Different reviewers can assign different outcomes to the same boundary measurement; the plan cannot yet support a reproducible pass/fail/inconclusive decision.

Retain the required deadline and choose an explicit decision rule. If using a three-way uncertainty-aware rule, pass when measured latency plus U is at most 200 ms, fail when measured latency minus U is greater than 200 ms, and otherwise mark inconclusive. Alternatively specify a conservative rejection policy and distinguish rejection from demonstrated nonconformance. Recheck 199/201 ms with nonzero uncertainty. Preserve this frozen first output and assess any repair as a new revision.

This finding lowers criterion 5 to 1. It does not invalidate the correctly preserved requirement, historical report, recovery correction or the rest of the planned evidence work. The issue is contradictory categories without explicit precedence; no single statistical decision rule is imposed as a universal requirement.

## Comparison limits

- The reviewer had no authorship role and did not consult author self-evaluations, but arm labels were visible; assessment was independent, not blinded.
- Each output was scored against its case and frozen rubric before a paired comparison was written. All scores use 0 missing/material contradiction, 1 partial/consequential gap, 2 coherent/supported; no winner is required.
- Multiple cases share each producer conversation. They are correlated outputs, not independent replications; case ordering and cross-case exposure can affect performance.
- Packet restrictions were instruction based. Global host instructions, available-skill metadata and general memory-summary metadata were visible, including in the none arm. None means no opened packet skill text, not absence of all prior engineering context.
- Exact model identities, model settings, token counts and costs were unavailable in the supplied run records. No token, cost, model or inference-speed comparison is supported.
- Observed start/end intervals overlap because cases were processed together; they cannot be treated as per-case inference runtimes.
- Predeclared inputs and supplied facts make these task-specific descriptive smoke observations. Small matched subsets, a coarse ceiling and no repeated/randomized runs do not support causal skill-effect or general-superiority claims.
- Reviewer hash checks establish correspondence among current bytes, manifests and the recorded hashes. They do not prove continuous immutability or independently reconstruct all producer tool usage/access history.
- Artifact consistency and plan quality are not executed controller, water-system, service, network or operational evidence. Proposed modern controls remain unvalidated.
