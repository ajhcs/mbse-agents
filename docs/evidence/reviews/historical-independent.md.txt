## F1 — [P3] Separate the Herald report date from its addendum — closed

Original location: `examples/historical/herald-free-enterprise/analysis.json:24`, propagated to `docs/sources-historical.md:198` and the case README. `publication_date: 1987-09` took the date of a correction addendum as the date of the original report.

The [cited scan](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/54c1704ce5274a15b6000025/FormalInvestigation_HeraldofFreeEnterprise-MSA1894.pdf), front matter, states first publication in 1987 and third impression in 1988. September 1987 dates the addendum concerning paragraph 19.6. The court decision is dated 24 July 1987; [Hansard that day, column 678](https://api.parliament.uk/historic-hansard/commons/1987/jul/24/herald-of-free-enterprise), confirms the court delivered its report that day. The defect affects bibliographic precision, not HG-14-F1/F2 or their teaching implications.

The author changed publication to `1987`, added the front-matter locator, and explicitly distinguished the 1988 impression and September 1987 addendum in the source limits. I independently read the revised bytes, confirmed that only the three expected files changed, and reran the local gallery and generated-freshness checks. **Closed.**

## Connected-bundle review

All source-fact CSV rows match their canonical JSON facts and attribution. Every interface is explicitly `new_retrospective_reconstruction`; each change has `synthetic_transfer_exercise` plus `historical_provenance: false`. All three claim registers distinguish one narrow `source_supported` claim, one `unsupported_extension`, and one `proposed_unexecuted` claim. No claim status asserts an unprovided test execution. The relation views use existing teaching IDs and cover all 11 interface IDs across the bundles. They are expressly information-flow aids, not formal SysML or executed models.

- **Hubble:** I1/I3 connect reference geometry and as-assembled evidence; I2 preserves conflicting evidence; I4 connects the production result to acceptance. C2 is a deliberate unsupported independence assertion, not an answer to accept. C3's replacement-fixture claim remains unverified. CH1 and the task ask for a discriminating check and an uncertainty-aware acceptance statement rather than a vote among instruments.
- **Mars Climate Orbiter:** I1 semantic interpretation and I2 handover are explicitly affected by CH1; I3 retains operational discrepancy escalation. C2 prevents unit metadata being treated as sufficient proof of navigation, and C3 supplies an unexecuted rejection claim. The task requires valid, unknown, missing and contradictory-metadata cases with an oracle and no invented pass. The old numeric-only path makes the transfer task more than a renamed historical summary.
- **Heathrow:** I1/I4 distinguish training facilities from demonstrated staff performance; I2/I3 connect organizational exception handling and frontline concerns. C2 prevents attendance/completion from closing readiness; C3 remains an unperformed rehearsal. CH1 and the task compare full, reduced and delayed opening using shared scenarios, roles, recovery authority and missing discriminating information.

The short case-level alternatives usually contrast a weak inference with a stronger evidence practice. They are useful introductory decision prompts; they are not calibrated trade studies or evidence that a learner can choose among closely competing designs. The three connected exercises add more substantive alternatives and unresolved evidence. This is a scope limitation, not an actionable correctness finding or a request for generic extra checklists.
