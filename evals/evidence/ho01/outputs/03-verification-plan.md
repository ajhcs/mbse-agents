## Configuration and acceptance controls

Before an activity is executable, record its approved requirement/design revision; decision dispositions; hardware wiring/sensor/driver/motor/vent/end-switch/indicator identifiers and revisions; firmware, build and configuration hashes; network topology/settings; temperature packet schema; timer/clock assumptions; load/weather envelope; instrument identity/calibration/uncertainty; fixture and procedure version; operator roles; observation channels; and deviations. A software-only setup must be labeled with its model scope and must not be used to claim hardware stopping behavior.

Observe both command/inhibit signals and physical vent movement where stopping or opening/closing is claimed. Timestamp sensor acquisition when available, receipt/acceptance, timer events, stop actuation, driver electrical response, physical motion and fault visibility. Use a defined common measurement time reference and uncertainty budget; do not assume unsynchronized clocks are comparable. Temperature boundary offsets and measurement tolerances must be selected from the resolved sensor contract, not arbitrary constants.

For each activity below, “acceptance” is the intended criterion. A D-ID indicates missing approval/input. These cases remain planned and acceptance-blocked until the decision and numeric/electrical contract are resolved; they cannot produce an unconditional pass in their present form. Proposed future evidence names are templates, not existing artifacts.
