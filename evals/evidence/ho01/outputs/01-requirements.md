# Requirements register

Baseline: GH-DRAFT-01. Source wording and locators are preserved in [00-review-guide.md](00-review-guide.md). Every record is unapproved. “Shall” expresses proposed requirement wording, not approval or proof of feasibility.

## Interpretation and measurement rules

- System boundary: controller, temperature acquisition interface, local stop interface, motor driver, vent actuator/end switches, fault indication and dashboard interface. The shared network, operator and weather are interacting external entities. Exact sensor, vent and power arrangements await D-11.
- `T` is an accepted inside-temperature reading in degrees Celsius. Whether a reading is valid, fresh and attributable to the inside sensor awaits D-01/D-05. A nominal 1 s stream does not establish uninterrupted physical temperature between samples.
- Opening uses `T >= 32 deg C` and resets on every accepted `T < 32 deg C`, including one brief dip. No smoothing or debounce may hide a valid dip under N-01.
- Closing uses the strict threshold `T < 28 deg C` and 60 s from N-01. Whether the interval must be uninterrupted, and how a rise or an invalid sample affects it, await D-02. No opening-reset rule is silently extended to closing.
- Timer start, sensor-time versus reception-time semantics, maximum sample gap, quantization, accuracy and acceptance tolerance await D-01/D-02/D-05/D-10. Architecture suggestions are provisional until these decisions are resolved.
- “Stop motion” is a required outcome. Disabling the driver is a mechanism; it does not prove physical arrest. D-04 separates local demand-to-inhibit latency from inhibit-to-physical-stop time. D-05 resolves missing-data timing. Neither threshold provides a supported universal 50 ms bound.

## Baseline review findings

No source obligation has been dropped. N-01 decomposes into three records without weakening the brief-dip opening reset; N-02 yields override and explicit resumption; N-03 yields stopping, fault indication and no automatic recovery; N-04 yields remote intent and network-independent local stop. RQ-09 remains non-measurable until scope is agreed. Timing tolerances, physical stop limits, sampling interpretation and weather envelope remain approval blockers. Candidate end switches and other proposed mechanisms are architecture choices, not retroactively attributed to the owner. There is no supported reliability target, regulatory obligation, hazard class or 50 ms deadline in this input.
