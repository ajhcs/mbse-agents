# HG-10 review guide

This guide evaluates reasoning in the new exercise, not historical blame or
a claim that a different decision would certainly have succeeded.

- Preserve the distinction between completion of a briefing and demonstrated
  performance under representative conditions.
- HG-10-C2 is unsupported because departmental readiness can coexist with
  missing handover and recovery capability.
- HG-10-C3 cannot be promoted from proposed to executed merely by creating a
  rehearsal plan.
- A useful fallback includes available resources, activation authority,
  customer consequences, and recovery criteria.
- The original report supplies context; it does not prove that one proposed
  opening strategy would have prevented the historical disruption.
