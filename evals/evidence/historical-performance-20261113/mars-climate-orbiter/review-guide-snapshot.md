# HG-05 review guide

This guide evaluates reasoning in the new exercise, not historical blame or
a claim that a different decision would certainly have succeeded.

- Preserve HG-05-C1 as a narrow attributed historical finding.
- HG-05-C2 remains unsupported: new metadata cannot establish physical meaning
  or mission correctness by itself.
- HG-05-C3 remains planned until an identified implementation is exercised and
  outputs are inspected. A plausible rejection policy is not a test result.
- A credible disposition considers compatibility, explicit unknown states,
  quarantining disputed inputs, shared producer/consumer review, and the
  consequences of changing the format.
- Do not claim that this exercise proves how the original mission would have
  behaved after the change.
