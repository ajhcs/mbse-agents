# Proposed revision to HG-05-I1

Status: proposed, unexecuted, and unapproved. The original ID, producer, consumer, meaning, basis, and reconstruction status remain in `interfaces.proposed.csv`; this text is a proposed revision, not a historical specification.

**Exchanged item:** impulse quantity records from Ground-data producer to Navigation consumer. The contract must define scalar or vector components, sign/frame, event identity, time basis and integration interval, precision, and validity limits. These details are absent and remain release blockers where they affect the sample or downstream interpretation. No timing or accuracy target is invented here.

**Version recognition:** propose an explicit envelope identifier and exact schema version `new-v1`. Only versions listed in the released consumer registry are recognized. Validate schema before interpretation; reject unknown, missing, duplicate, or contradictory version identifiers. A filename, numeric magnitude, parse success, or supplier name does not identify a version. Never retry rejected new-format data through a permissive legacy parser.

**Unit semantics:** initially allow only the exact proposed token `N*s`, meaning newton-seconds of impulse, bound to the quantity and its frame/time definition. Other strings, empty/null/missing units, mixed metadata, or quantity/unit mismatches are rejected. These tokens are design proposals to be agreed, not existing standard schemas. Supporting another known unit requires a separately reviewed mapping and independent conversion samples; this review does not enable it implicitly.

**Legacy path:** numeric-only records cannot authenticate their own units. Disable direct numeric-only ingestion. A separate controlled `legacy-v0` adapter may be proposed only when a reviewed manifest binds each file's SHA256, producing release, original units, quantity definition, and derivation history. The adapter produces the same validated canonical representation and preserves the original bytes and conversion lineage. Unknown or unrecoverable provenance means quarantine, even if the values look plausible. Verified provenance is an eligibility condition, not proof of correct numerical processing.

**Acceptance and failure:** stage each file, validate the entire file and its envelope, and commit atomically only after applicable checks. Any unknown or contradictory item quarantines the whole file; do not partially add accepted records. Preserve bytes, hash, schema identifier, rejection code, and diagnostic context. Accepted-state identifiers and values must be unchanged after rejection. Recovery requires a new controlled input/revision and owner disposition, not an operator unit guess or relabeling the same bytes to silence an error.

**Semantic check:** the acceptance sample must compare the actual consumer interpretation against a quantity established independently of the exporter and its metadata. A recognized `N*s` label cannot reveal every incorrect number. The future test harness must detect the seeded metadata/quantity disagreement; general live detection needs an available independent observation or residual check and remains unestablished here. Unexplained residuals follow HG-05-I3; a parser acceptance cannot close HG-05-C2.

**Ownership:** the Navigation verification lead owns and controls the paired acceptance sample and its expected answers, with independent review of the derivation. The producer interface owner supplies originating quantity provenance and export configuration. The consumer integration owner implements and tests dispatch, validation, isolation, and state transitions. The development and operations leads jointly own handover under HG-05-I2. The integration lead confirms owners, acceptance scope, and review triggers. These are proposed responsibilities, not recorded appointments.

**Evidence record:** preserve input/output hashes, source-quantity provenance, expected intermediate and final quantities, unit transformations, frame/time interpretation, tolerance rationale, producer/adapter/consumer versions, procedure revision, pre/post accepted state, warnings, rejection/quarantine log, observed outcomes, deviations, reviewer decision and acceptance decision separately. No such execution record exists in this bundle.
