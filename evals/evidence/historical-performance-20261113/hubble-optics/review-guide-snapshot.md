# HG-03 review guide

This guide evaluates reasoning in the new exercise, not historical blame or
a claim that a different decision would certainly have succeeded.

- Distinguish repeated measurement, independent computation, and an independent
  physical reference. They address different failure mechanisms.
- HG-03-C2 is an unsupported independence claim even if both computers are
  functioning correctly.
- HG-03-C3 remains unverified until the replacement fixture itself is checked;
  a drawing or calibration certificate for another configuration is insufficient.
- Treat the auxiliary discrepancy as a question to resolve, not automatic proof
  that either instrument is correct.
- A strong response chooses a check based on discriminating power and
  uncertainty, not the prestige or number of agreeing instruments.
