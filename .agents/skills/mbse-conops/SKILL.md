# Develop a Concept of Operations

Describe how people and systems will accomplish the intended work. Use the operating evidence to distinguish current practice, including workarounds, from proposed operations. Keep operational needs visible separately from any chosen implementation. Identify the users, support organizations, external services, and environmental conditions that change the proposed operation.

Show who initiates, supervises, overrides, hands over, restores, and accepts the work where those decisions arise. Walk through normal use and the degraded, maintenance, or recovery conditions that could invalidate the concept. Check that each responsibility matches the staffing, authority, training, information, and equipment actually available at the time.

The ConOps is ready for review when operators and support staff can locate their decisions, the assumptions about external dependencies, and the conditions under which they can judge success. Tie consequential claims and conflicts to the scenarios they affect. Use [operational framing](references/operational-framing.md) for a broader ConOps or when the operating measures need definition.
