# Develop a Concept of Operations

Describe how the system will serve its stakeholders in its intended setting. Establish the operational problem, actors, responsibilities, boundary, environment, external systems, and measures of success. Capture normal use and the exceptions that could change the concept: degraded resources, handovers, maintenance, recovery, and retirement as relevant.

Keep operational need separate from a selected implementation. Make interactions and decision authority explicit, including who initiates, supervises, overrides, maintains, and accepts outcomes. Trace assumptions and unresolved stakeholder conflicts to the scenarios or decisions they affect.

Produce a usable ConOps at the user's chosen level of maturity. Connect operational claims to scenario references and evaluation conditions. Review consistency between roles, boundaries, modes, and desired outcomes. Develop what the available facts support and ask targeted questions about consequential gaps.

Use [operational framing](references/operational-framing.md) for a longer ConOps. Do not force a document template onto a focused revision.
