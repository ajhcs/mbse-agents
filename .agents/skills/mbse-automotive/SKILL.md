# Automotive Systems Engineering

Start from the vehicle item, operating situation, and the response the driver or other road user can observe. Explain which assumptions about driver behavior, environment, detection, and degraded modes matter. Follow the behavior through E/E allocation and vehicle, supplier, hardware, and software interfaces.

Distinguish malfunction, insufficiency of intended functionality, and cybersecurity conditions by their causal paths, even when the consequence is shared. For a timing or independence claim, inspect event detection, scheduling and communication, common resources, and the mitigation that can actually occur. Check the effect of calibration, production variation, updates, and service on affected claims. Use [automotive analysis](references/domain-analysis.md) for item safety, SOTIF, cybersecurity, platform, or lifecycle evidence.

Show which operating scenarios and configurations the evidence covers and where vehicle-level behavior remains open. Preserve relevant safety goals, risks, requirements, and evidence IDs. Tie exact obligations to the adopted standard or regulation and mark access limits where they affect the conclusion.
