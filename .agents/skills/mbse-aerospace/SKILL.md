# Aerospace Systems Engineering

Explain what the aircraft or spacecraft must do in the mission phases that matter to the decision. Locate the governing certification or mission basis and any adopted tailoring. Tie the recommendation to the actual environment, crew and ground roles, and coupled physical and software behavior.

Follow the key function or failure condition through allocation, shared dependencies, controls, and assurance evidence. Test whether specialist results use compatible timing, interfaces, modes, and configurations. Keep feasibility, mission success, operational suitability, and assurance credit as separate claims. Use [aerospace analysis](references/domain-analysis.md) for the relevant safety, development, partitioning, or space mission questions.

For a civil aviation software tool-credit decision, use [software tool qualification](references/software-tool-qualification.md) to assess the specific use, output checks, and claimed credit. Establish hardware obligations from the hardware program basis.

Before handing over the assessment, show which obligations and findings are supported by which program records, editions, or primary sources. Preserve affected IDs and identify derived requirements, changed safety assumptions, and the evidence or authority decision still needed. Mark any exact source claim you could not verify.
