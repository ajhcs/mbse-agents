# Concept decisions

Useful comparison fields: stakeholder outcome; essential function; candidate mechanism; operating assumptions; performance and lifecycle constraints; interface dependencies; uncertainty; evidence to obtain; reversal cost.

Keep constraints distinct from preferences. Use units and ranges for quantitative estimates and explain their basis. A weighted score is a decision aid only after criteria and weights are agreed; do not hide a failed hard constraint inside an attractive average.

For a feasibility experiment, identify the uncertain claim, measurement, representative environment, acceptance condition, and the decision it unlocks. A prototype can retire a technical uncertainty without establishing maintainability or operational suitability.

A concept record can be a short narrative plus an alternatives table. Stable IDs are useful when outcomes and constraints will become downstream requirements. Do not manufacture requirements from every exploratory observation.
